Definition of Hazard (Ref SMSM Ed. 05 Rev 00 Chapter 2.1.1)

English (Original):

"Hazard shall be identified in any operation of a Citilink Indonesia operation, not only operational area, but also to include airworthiness, back office and third parties. Hazard identification is everybody's responsibility, any unit/person shall identify the hazard within their scope during their duty as a supporting unit on Citilink Indonesia operation and report to Corporate Safety, Security & Quality Group Head..."

Bahasa Indonesia (Terjemahan):

Bahaya (hazard) harus diidentifikasi dalam setiap operasi Citilink Indonesia, tidak hanya di area operasional, tetapi juga mencakup kelaikudaraan, back office, dan pihak ketiga. Identifikasi bahaya adalah tanggung jawab semua orang...
